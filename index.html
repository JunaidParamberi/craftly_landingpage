What is Craftly.AI?

Craftly.AI is an all-in-one AI-powered business management platform built for freelancers, consultants, and independent professionals. It replaces separate invoicing, CRM, time tracking, proposal, and client portal tools with one intelligent workspace.

Is Craftly.AI free for freelancers?

Yes. Craftly.AI offers a free forever plan with up to 3 clients, 5 invoices per month, and time tracking. Paid plans start at $19/month for unlimited clients, invoices, and AI agents.

Craftly.AI includes six specialized AI agents: Document Writer, Finance Analyst, Communication Drafter, Project Intelligence, Pricing Advisor, and Business Strategist. They automate follow-ups, invoice reminders, proposal drafts, and cashflow forecasting.

Does Craftly.AI replace FreshBooks, Honeybook, or Notion?

Craftly.AI consolidates invoicing, CRM, time tracking, proposals, client portals, and finance dashboards into one platform — reducing the need for 6–8 separate freelancer tools and subscriptions.
