Questions people ask

What is CraftlyAI?

One app to run your business: clients, projects, quotes, invoices, payments, expenses and follow-ups, with an assistant called Kraft that does the busywork.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan covers 3 clients and 5 documents a month, with no card. Starter is AED 69 a month when you need more.

Will Kraft send things without me?

No. Kraft drafts quotes, invoices and emails and can record payments, but anything that sends, charges or deletes waits for your approval first.

Can I use it from Claude?

Yes, on every plan. Connect CraftlyAI to Claude and ask it to look things up or prepare work. Sends, payments and deletes land in Approvals, one by one or as a batch.

Does it handle UAE VAT?

Yes. Add your TRN once and every invoice shows VAT correctly. It also supports Saudi ZATCA QR codes, UK VAT numbers and Indian GST.

Can it replace FreshBooks, HoneyBook or my spreadsheet?

For most businesses, yes: invoicing, clients, quotes and proposals, time tracking, a client portal and expenses in one place. Bring past invoices over with Claude in one go.
